## About Artan Records

**Artan Records** is the independent home, label, archive, and digital infrastructure surrounding the music and artistic work of **Artan Davoodi**.

The project exists to establish a durable, independent structure for music releases, catalog management, artistic presentation, public communication, metadata, archives, distribution, collaborations, and the long-term preservation of the work.

It is not intended to function merely as a record-label website.

Artan Records is being built as an evolving artistic infrastructure capable of supporting the complete lifecycle of a body of work:

**creation → documentation → release → distribution → communication → preservation**

The guiding principle is:

> **Build the infrastructure around the art without allowing the infrastructure to dictate the art.**

# Artistic Philosophy

Artan Davoodi's current musical language centers on:

**Neoclassical / minimalist / cinematic composition**

The work favors restraint rather than spectacle.

Core qualities include:

- simplicity
- intimacy
- silence
- fragility
- impermanence
- memory
- solitude
- emotional restraint
- physical acoustic presence
- space between notes
- deliberate incompleteness
- organic imperfections

The music should not be reduced to generic *relaxing piano*, background music, or commercial cinematic music.

Its identity is fundamentally compositional and artistic.

A central principle is:

> **Say as much as possible with as little as necessary.**

Simplicity does not mean removing meaning.

It means removing what prevents the essential idea from being heard.

The current production language favors:

- intimate felt and upright piano

# Current Primary Project — Amber

**Amber** is the current primary artistic project.

It is conceived as a conceptual neoclassical / ambient piano cycle.

Current architecture:

### 01 — Ochre

Warmth, organic decay, materiality, earth before frost.

### 02 — Drift

Movement without anchoring, gradual cooling, emotional detachment.

### 03 — Brittle

**Key:** G minor

Fracture, exposed nerve, glass-like instability.

### 04 — Haze

**Key:** E-flat major

Mist, diffusion, obscured contours, watercolor-like harmonic bleeding.

### 05 — Frost

**Key:** C minor

Crystalline stasis, rime, immobility, cold resolution without conventional comfort.

### 06 — Coda / Concluding Resolution

The closing movement of the conceptual cycle.

The album moves from:

**warmth → materiality → movement → fracture → diffusion → frost → resolution**

# Artistic Background

Music was present from childhood.

Artan's father was a professional musician and producer, creating an early connection between music, recording, and everyday life.

Early experimentation included violin and keyboard.

During adolescence in Iran, music files were exchanged between mobile phones using infrared technology before streaming became commonplace.

Early creative experimentation also included:

- keyboard beats
- lyric writing
- mobile-phone vocal recording
- experimentation with acoustic spaces

Before knowing the technical concept of reverb, Artan discovered that recording inside a bathroom changed the spatial character of his voice and intentionally used the room as part of the recording.

The underlying principle remains relevant:

> **Use available space, texture, and limitations as part of the music.**

# Decision Framework

Before implementing a new feature, page, campaign, service, repository, account, integration, or workflow, ask:

1. Does this serve the music?
2. Does it strengthen ownership or independence?
3. Does it make the catalog more durable?
4. Does it improve discoverability or accessibility?
5. Does it reduce future operational friction?
6. Does it preserve the artistic identity?
7. Is it actually necessary now?

If not:

**defer it.**
