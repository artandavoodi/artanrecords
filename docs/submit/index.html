Three steps to share your work

01

Let us hear the work

Introduce the artist behind the recording. Share a listening link and tell us whether it is a single, EP or album, who contributed, and what you hope to release.

A private streaming link is enough. Keep masters and project files with you.

02

A considered review

We listen and read the context you share. Each enquiry is considered individually, with the recording and your intentions at the centre of the conversation.

If there is a fit, we can discuss the project. Submission is not acceptance or a release agreement.

03

A clear next step

Open the form below, introduce yourself and tell us what you are looking for. Share only work and links you have permission to send.

No attachments are needed. Do not include identity documents, bank details or tax information.

Share the work
