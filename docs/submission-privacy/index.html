Submission privacy

Effective date: 2026-10-16Last updated: 2026-10-28

Your enquiry

Artanrecords uses your name, contact details, introduction and submitted links to review your work and respond. Sending an enquiry does not publish a profile or create an offer of representation.

Processing and storage

Artist submissions pass through our Supabase service to the existing Google service for verification, private Google Sheet storage and an email notification to our collaboration inbox, hosted by iCloud. A signed server-to-server handoff records artist enquiries in the shared Artan Supabase backend for authorized Artanrecords reviewers in Control Center. If that handoff temporarily fails, the saved Sheet row remains pending for retry. Contact enquiries use the existing Google form service directly. Applications and review notes are never added to the public website repository.

Optional artist search

Searching sends the artist name you enter to connected streaming platforms through our search service. Search results and selected profile links are listening references, not proof of identity or ownership. Selected platform names, artist names, identifiers and links are included with your submission; provider portraits are displayed only where supplied and are not copied into your artist profile. You can skip search and enter your name and a listening link manually.

Verification

Opening the form loads Google reCAPTCHA to help prevent spam and abuse. It processes browser and interaction information and uses a necessary verification cookie. Verification is required before an enquiry can be accepted.

What to share

Share listening links, not private masters, project files or sensitive documents. Include only work and information you have permission to share.

Retention and requests

Closed enquiries are removed from the active enquiry sheet by a daily cleanup once 30 days have elapsed after their recorded Declined or Closed status. Active conversations and legacy rows without a recorded closure date are not eligible. Notification emails and provider-held history require separate review; this is not a promise of automatic deletion from every provider. You can request access, correction or deletion by contacting collaborate@artanrecords.com. Do not send additional sensitive documents with your request.

External services

Google processes verification and form data, and iCloud hosts the notification inbox. Their own privacy notices apply to their services. This notice describes the current enquiry process; project-specific data handling is agreed separately.

Your choices

You can contact Artanrecords by email instead of using the form. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing or create an artist account. Include only information relevant to the work and obtain permission before sharing another person's private details. Requests about previously submitted information are reviewed individually.

Contact

Artanrecords is an independent label founded by Artan Davoodi and is not currently a registered company. For enquiry privacy questions, contact collaborate@artanrecords.com.
