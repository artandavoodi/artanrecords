When you will hear from us

We aim to respond within 14 working days. This is a review target, not a guaranteed deadline; some projects may take longer.

How we review

We listen to the submitted work and consider its context, rights, credits and the support you are seeking. Share links you have permission to send. The first submission does not require masters, identity documents, banking details or a Studio account.

Partnerships and Studio

If there is a fit, we discuss Silver, Gold or Platinum based on the agreed scope of support, not an artist ranking. Silver includes Studio; Gold and Platinum include Studio Pro under the partnership terms. An enquiry, streaming-profile selection or proposed tier does not activate a subscription or create an agreement.

What happens next

An accepted proposal moves to account verification and a separate partnership review and agreement. Any release, services and commercial terms must be confirmed before work begins. Submitting this form does not publish music, sign a contract or charge a fee.
