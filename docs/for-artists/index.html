The music comes first

Send your artist name, a short introduction and a listening link. Singles, EPs and albums can begin here. We listen and review enquiries individually: this is a conversation, not automatic distribution.

Share links, not private masters or project files.

A clear next step, together

If there is a fit, we discuss your project, credits, artwork and release plans. The scope, responsibilities and commercial terms must be agreed before an engagement begins.

Submission is not acceptance. Promotion is not automatically included.
