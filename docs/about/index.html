About

Artanrecords is a curated independent record label founded by Artan Davoodi in 2025. We listen first, then agree the right scope of release support. Artists retain their masters; accepted standard releases have no upfront base distribution fee.

Founded in 2025 by Artan Davoodi, Artanrecords supports artists through release administration, catalogue presentation and creative collaboration. Each partnership begins with human review and an agreed scope of work.

Founded
2025

Our public catalogue connects recordings with the people who made them. Dedicated artist and release pages bring together artwork, credits, release information and links to streaming services. Artist introductions and creative proposals are reviewed individually before any engagement is agreed.

Mission

The infrastructure serves the art. We welcome music and creative collaborations through a considered, human-reviewed process. Each project begins with the work and the person behind it, not an automatic upload pipeline.

Our mission is to give artists and collaborators a clear, considered way to develop and present their work. We value accurate credits, coherent release information and respectful collaboration across music and visual practice.

We begin with listening and conversation. Release preparation and creative support are discussed for each project, with responsibilities and terms agreed before work begins. We do not treat every artist or recording as the same kind of project.

Leadership

Artan Davoodi

Founder

Artan Davoodi founded Artanrecords in 2025 to create an independent home for recordings and creative collaboration. A musician, songwriter and producer, he brings an artist's perspective to the label's direction and the way projects are reviewed and presented.
