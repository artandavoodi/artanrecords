About

Artan Records is an independent record label built around artists and their recordings. We bring music, credits, artwork and release information together, giving the work a clear and lasting home.

Founded
2025

The infrastructure serves the art. We welcome music and creative collaborations through a considered, human-reviewed process. Each project begins with the work and the person behind it, not an automatic upload pipeline.

Founded in 2025 by Artan Davoodi, Artan Records began with his recordings and is opening a wider conversation with artists and collaborators. The catalog is a beginning, not a boundary.
