Your artist website and store

An artist-first home for your work, with a connected store and one place to manage it. Explore the direction and what is available now.

Your work. Your identity.

Your artist page brings your biography, music, releases and public links together. Studio is where you manage your artist information and choose what to share. Publishing requires an approved username and your choice to make the profile public; creating an account alone does not publish your information or establish a label partnership.

A website and a store, connected

Your artist website tells the story of your work. Your store presents the things you offer: releases, editions and merchandise. They serve different purposes, but should feel like parts of the same artist identity.

We are developing a shared storefront system that keeps those experiences connected without turning your public website into an administration panel.

Manage your work in Studio

Studio is the management space; your public page is the presentation. You can manage your profile and prepare private release and store drafts there. Private drafts are not public products or a live shop.

The planned store tools will bring products, collections, variants, inventory, orders, delivery, reporting and domain settings into that same workspace. These capabilities are being developed in stages; they are not all available today.

Open Studio

One store. More than one address.

The planned Artanrecords storefront address is artanrecords.com/{username}/shop/. A verified custom domain would provide another address for that same store, not a second product catalogue, inventory or set of earnings.

Your artist identity and store records should stay connected even if your domain changes. The Artanrecords address is intended to remain a platform fallback, with any preferred-domain routing handled as part of a reviewed setup.

Use your own domain

Custom domains are planned for artist websites and stores. Connecting one will require ownership verification, secure hosting setup and an agreed migration plan. Domain registration, renewals and responsibility for access must be agreed separately; a Studio account does not purchase or connect a domain.

If you already have a website or store, keep it running while the replacement is prepared and reviewed. No domain should move until the new experience, content and routing have been checked and the change has been approved.

A shared platform. An individual presence.

Your images, writing, releases, collections and visual choices belong to your artistic identity. The shared platform is being designed to support that individuality while providing consistent tools for managing and presenting the work. You should not need a separate administration system for every public address.

What is available now

Studio supports accounts, artist-profile management and private release, product and collection drafts. Public artist pages use the information you explicitly select for publication on an approved profile.

Shared public shops, connected custom domains, checkout, order management and live earnings or withdrawals are not yet available. A payout application is a request for review, not confirmation of an active payment account. Prices, store revenue shares and withdrawal rules will need to be defined before those services launch; do not assume they follow recording partnership terms.

Start with your artist profile

Prepare your biography, images and public links in Studio. If you already run a website or store, you can discuss your existing setup with Artanrecords before making changes. Keep bank details, tax identifiers and identity documents out of public profiles and enquiry forms.
