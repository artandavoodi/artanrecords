Good music brings people together

Introduce an artist to Artanrecords. A referral programme for creative services is being prepared.

Know an artist we should hear?

Point them toward our submission page. Every artist is considered on their work and the scope of a potential partnership, not on who introduced them. A referral never guarantees acceptance.

More room for the work

The planned programme would recognise qualifying artist introductions with credits toward approved promotion, artwork or website services. It is not open for earning or spending credits yet. Award values and qualifying milestones will be explained before launch.

Clear from the beginning

Programme terms will define eligibility, attribution, awards, redemption, expiry, cancellations and disputes. Service credits are separate from recording royalties and do not change a release agreement. Cash conversion and credit withdrawals are not currently available.

Start with the music

Artists can explore our partnerships and share a listening link without paying an upfront base distribution fee for an accepted standard release. Additional services require an agreed scope. No one needs to join a referral programme to be considered.
