A partnership around your music

Three ways to work with Artanrecords. Zero upfront cost for accepted standard releases, with responsibilities agreed before work begins.

Your work. A considered next step.

Silver, Gold and Platinum describe our involvement, not the quality of your music. Choose a preference or ask us to recommend a partnership. We review the project, our capacity and the scope before offering an agreement.

What zero upfront means

Accepted standard releases carry no upfront base distribution fee. You provide finished release-ready masters, artwork, metadata, credits, contributor information and the necessary rights and clearances. Additional services and expenses must be agreed in advance as included, artist-paid or recoupable. Nothing is added silently.

Your masters remain yours

Our partnership model leaves master ownership with the artist. The agreement defines the licence, term, territory and share of eligible recording-side Net Receipts. Publishing, songwriting, live income, merchandise and unrelated recordings are separate unless expressly agreed.

From introduction to agreement

A preference is not acceptance or an automatic contract. After review, we may propose the requested partnership, recommend another scope or decline. Services, revenue allocation, approved expenses, reporting and departure arrangements are confirmed before onboarding.
