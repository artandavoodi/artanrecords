Platinum — Our most involved artist partnership

For selected projects where Artanrecords takes a deeper role in release leadership, presentation and artist development.

A deeper role in the work

Platinum builds on Gold with an agreed label-led project plan, release-calendar coordination, campaign management, catalogue strategy and periodic artist-development reviews. Multi-release planning and higher-touch involvement must have a defined scope and realistic capacity. Requesting Platinum does not grant it automatically.

One considered presentation

The project may include expanded artwork direction, visual positioning, campaign narrative, editorial development and website creative direction. A dedicated website receives priority within the agreed scope, including press material, release archives and a defined handover. Third-party production is not automatically included.

Investment with clear terms

Custom campaign strategy is distinct from paid media spend. Any external investment must be agreed as label-funded and non-recoupable, artist-paid, or an approved capped recoupable expense. No advertising budget, advance, press coverage or commercial outcome is automatic.

A continuing conversation

Scheduled strategic reviews can cover positioning, future releases, audience development and relevant introductions. Sync, brand, live and licensing opportunities receive consideration only where available, with separate rights and terms where needed. The artist retains creative independence. Deeper Studio reporting is a future capability, not a current financial service.

What zero upfront means

Accepted standard releases carry no upfront base distribution fee. You provide finished release-ready masters, artwork, metadata, credits, contributor information and the necessary rights and clearances. Additional services and expenses must be agreed in advance as included, artist-paid or recoupable. Nothing is added silently.

Your masters remain yours

Our partnership model leaves master ownership with the artist. The agreement defines the licence, term, territory and share of eligible recording-side Net Receipts. Publishing, songwriting, live income, merchandise and unrelated recordings are separate unless expressly agreed.

From introduction to agreement

A preference is not acceptance or an automatic contract. After review, we may propose the requested partnership, recommend another scope or decline. Services, revenue allocation, approved expenses, reporting and departure arrangements are confirmed before onboarding.

Can you recommend a partnership?

Yes. Share your music and what you want to build. We can recommend a scope after reviewing the project. No preference creates an entitlement or agreement.

How is recording income shared?

The written offer explains the artist and Artanrecords shares of contractually defined eligible master-side Net Receipts, including any approved deductions and expenses. Review those terms before signing. Partnership names never override a signed agreement.

What happens when the agreement ends?

The agreement must define notice, licence expiry, final reporting, outstanding amounts, catalogue changes and any website or domain handover. Distribution migration needs coordination; continuity of service data or playlists cannot be guaranteed.

Are marketing results guaranteed?

No. Pitching, promotion and opportunity consideration cannot guarantee streams, editorial placement, press, growth or earnings. Publishing and other rights administration require separate terms.
