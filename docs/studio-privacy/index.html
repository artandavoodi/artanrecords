Privacy and cookies

How Artanrecords Studio handles account information and your choices.

Effective date: 2026-10-19Last updated: 2026-10-19

Your accountCookies and similar technologiesYour choices

Your account

Studio account information is used to provide access, maintain your artist profile and respond to your requests. Creating an account does not grant access to another artist's work or financial records. Profile photographs and unreleased submissions are not public catalogue content.

Cookies and similar technologies

Essential browser storage supports security and your consent choices. Optional preference memory stores appearance, contrast, palette and sidebar choices across visits only when permitted. Declining optional storage keeps interface changes in session storage until the tab session ends. Analytics are not connected. These preferences do not currently synchronize to an artist account. See the Cookie Policy for storage categories and controls.

Your choices

For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact contact@artanrecords.com. Some records may need to be retained to meet contractual or legal obligations. Account and payment services will be described here when connected; no banking or tax information should be submitted through public forms.
