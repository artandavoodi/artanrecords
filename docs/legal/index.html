Enquiries, not agreements

The public form is a way to introduce your work. Submitting an enquiry does not create an artist account, an offer of representation or a distribution agreement.

Respect the work

Only share material and information you are authorised to share. Questions about a recording, credit or rights concern can be sent to contact@artanrecords.com. Include the relevant release link and a clear explanation.

Project terms

Rights, responsibilities and commercial terms belong in the agreement for the specific engagement. This website is not a substitute for that agreement.
