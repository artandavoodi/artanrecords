Enquiries, not agreements

The public form is an introduction to Artan Records: a way to share a recording, describe a project or propose a collaboration. It begins a review and a possible conversation, not a contractual relationship.

Submitting an enquiry does not create an artist account, an offer of representation, a distribution agreement or an approved artist profile. A receipt confirmation means your enquiry was received; it does not mean your work has been accepted.

Tell us who you are, what you are sharing and what you would like to discuss. Use listening or portfolio links rather than private masters or sensitive documents. If you represent someone else, explain your role and share only information you are authorised to provide.

Any proposed engagement needs its own discussion and agreement before work begins. Do not assume that a submission includes distribution, promotion, playlist placement or commercial results.

Respect the work

Share only recordings, images, lyrics, credits and other material you are authorised to share. A publicly accessible link does not by itself establish permission to use or release the material.

Identify collaborators and any known third-party contributions, including samples, cover compositions, photographs and artwork. Explain any permissions that still need to be resolved rather than presenting them as confirmed. Do not submit confidential material belonging to another person.

The public catalogue presents approved release information and links to listening services. Access to an image or recording through this website is not a statement that it is free to reuse, redistribute or adapt. For permission questions, contact the relevant rights holder or ask Artan Records to help identify the appropriate contact.

For a credit correction or rights concern, email contact@artanrecords.com with the release link, the material concerned, your relationship to it and a clear explanation. Avoid sending identity documents or unrelated personal information in the initial message.

Project terms

Each engagement needs a clear scope suited to the project. This website describes the label and its enquiry process; it is not the agreement governing a release or collaboration.

Before work begins, the parties should clarify the services involved, responsibilities, deliverables, approvals and any proposed timetable. Rights ownership and any permission to distribute or use recordings, compositions, artwork or an artist identity must be addressed expressly.

Where relevant, the project discussion should also cover costs, revenue allocation, reporting, payment arrangements, duration and how the engagement may end. These are subjects for a project-specific agreement, not standard terms promised by this page.

Artan Records does not publish a universal royalty percentage, payout schedule or promotion guarantee here. Ask for clarification of anything you do not understand before accepting an agreement, and seek independent legal or accounting advice where appropriate.

Personal information

Artist enquiries ask for contact details and project information so Artan Records can review the work and respond. Provide only what is relevant to that conversation, and obtain permission before including another person's private information.

Do not use the public enquiry form to send identity documents, bank details, tax information, private contracts or confidential masters. Secure onboarding for sensitive information is not provided by this public website.

The submission privacy notice explains the current form process, including the services used to handle enquiries and verification. External streaming and social links take you to other services, where their own notices and settings apply.

For a question about information you submitted, or to request a correction or deletion, contact contact@artanrecords.com and describe the enquiry using information you already shared. Do not send additional sensitive documents unless an appropriate process has been agreed. This overview does not replace the submission privacy notice or a project-specific agreement.
