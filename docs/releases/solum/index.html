Story

Solum grew after MONAD was made public, during a period of solitude, self-authorship, and a deeper examination of consciousness. Its nine instrumental works move through ideas of origin, presence, and inner formation. The title carries the weight of soul, solitude, and being deeply with oneself, shaped during the period following his father's death.
